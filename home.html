Welcome to Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical Engineering is the branch of engineering that deals with the design, manufacturing, operation, and maintenance of machines and mechanical systems.

Engineering the Future Through Innovation!

About Our Department

The Department of Mechanical Engineering provides students with knowledge of engineering principles, machine design, manufacturing technology, thermal engineering, and computer-aided design.

Our department focuses on theoretical learning, practical training, teamwork, and the development of innovative engineering solutions.

Mechanical Workshops

Workshops help students gain practical experience in manufacturing processes and machine operations.

  • Fitting Workshop
  • Welding Workshop
  • Smithy and Forging Workshop
  • Carpentry Workshop
  • Lathe and Machining Workshop
  • Foundry Workshop

Students learn to handle tools, understand manufacturing methods, and follow workshop safety procedures.

CAD/CAM Laboratory

CAD stands for Computer-Aided Design, while CAM stands for Computer-Aided Manufacturing.

The CAD/CAM laboratory helps students design mechanical components, create 3D models, prepare engineering drawings, and understand computer-controlled manufacturing.

Laboratory Activities

  • 2D Engineering Drawing
  • 3D Part Modelling
  • Assembly Design
  • Computer-Aided Manufacturing
  • CNC Programming
  • Product Design and Simulation
